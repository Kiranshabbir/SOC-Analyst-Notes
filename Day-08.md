## 3. New words
- Topology: how devices are arranged in a network.
- Star: all devices connect to one center (my home router).
- Bus: all devices share one main cable.
- Ring: devices are connected in a circle.
- Mesh: devices are connected to many other devices.
- Bandwidth: how much data a connection can carry.
- Throughput: how much data really moves through the connection.
