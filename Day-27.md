## 2. What I remembered without help
- Get-Process shows:  many processes, like chrome, explorer, svchost, and Sysmon64
- Get-Service shows: services like Windows Update, Windows Defender, and Task Scheduler, with their status
- The CIA Triad has 3 parts: Confidentiality (keep data safe), Integrity (keep data unchanged), Availability (keeps data available when needed).
- A threat actor is a person or group that attacks computers or networks. Two types are cybercriminals and nation-state actors. 
- Three malware types: virus, worm, trojan. - - Virus: attaches itself to a file and spreads when the file is opened or shared.
- Worm: spreads by itself across a network, without needing a person to open anything.
- Trojan: hides inside software that looks normal or useful, but is actually harmful.
