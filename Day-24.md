# Day 24 - Threat actors and attack types
Date: Nov 19, 2026


## 1. What I learned
- A threat actor is a person or group that attacks computers, like hackers, nation-states, or insiders.
- An attack type is the method they use, like phishing or DDoS.
- A nation-state attacker works for a government.
- A hacktivist attacks for a political or social reason, not money.
- An insider threat comes from someone already inside the company, like an employee.
- Organized crime groups attack for money.

## 3. New words
- Phishing: tricking someone into giving information or clicking a bad link.
- Brute force: trying many passwords until one works.
- SQL injection: putting bad code into a website's search or login box to steal data.
- DDoS: sending too much traffic to a website so it cannot work.
- Man-in-the-middle: secretly listening to or changing data between two people talking.
