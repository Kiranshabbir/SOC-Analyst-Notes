# Day 6 - MAC Addresses, Switches, and Routers
  Date: Nov 1, 2026

## 1. What I learned
- A MAC address is the hardware ID of my network card.
- A switch connects devices inside one network.
- A router connects different networks and gives me internet.

## 2. What I did
- I watched videos about MAC addresses, switches, and routers.
- I opened Command Prompt and ran: ipconfig /all
- My MAC address (Physical Address, Wi-Fi section): AC-81-12-xx-xx-xx
- My Subnet Mask: 255.255.255.0
- I finished the TryHackMe rooms: Intro to LAN and DNS in Detail.
- A switch connects devices inside one LAN. A router connects my LAN to the internet (WAN).

 ## 3. New words
- MAC address: a unique hardware ID of a network card.
- Switch: connects devices in a local network.
- Router: connects networks and shares internet.
