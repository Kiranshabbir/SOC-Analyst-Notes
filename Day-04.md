 Day 4 - Networking Basics
 Date: Oct 30, 2026

 1. What I learned
- A network is two or more devices connected to share data.
- LAN means Local Area Network. It is a small network, like my home.
- WAN means Wide Area Network. It is a big network over long distances.
- The internet is the biggest WAN in the world.
- A client asks for something (my phone or laptop). A server gives it (like a website).

3. New words
- Network: devices connected together
- LAN: small network in one place
- WAN: big network over a long distance
- Internet: network of many networks
- Client: device that asks
- Server: computer that answers
