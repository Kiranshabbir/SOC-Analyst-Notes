# Day 9 - OSI and TCP/IP Models
  Date: Nov 4, 2026

## 1. What I learned
- The OSI model has 7 layers: Physical, Data Link, Network, Transport, Session, Presentation, Application.
- The TCP/IP model has 4 layers: Network Access, Internet, Transport, Application.
- Layers help us understand how data moves through a network.

## 3. New words
- OSI = Open Systems Interconnection
- TCP/IP = Transmission Control Protocol / Internet Protocol
- Layer: one step in how data moves through a network.
- Protocol: a set of rules for communication.
